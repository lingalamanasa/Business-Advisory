OUR HERITAGE

From Boutique Strategy to Global Advisory Partner

Founded in 2015 by former tier-one strategy partners and corporate CFOs, Stackly was born out of a determination to bridge the gap between academic consulting reports and actionable corporate execution.

Today, our firm oversees multi-million dollar corporate transformations across 18 regional hubs. We operate as discreet, trusted counselors to industry leaders seeking market supremacy, restructuring guidance, and generational enterprise value.
